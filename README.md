A two-line warning to a past self, written for the
[Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca)
competition on make.fun. Line one warns about one specific moment — a boss
asking you to stay late on your daughter's third birthday, the cake still
warm on the kitchen table. Line two traces what that moment cost: she
stopped asking you to come home, and the next ten years were spent learning
how to be invited back.

## The entry

> Don't say yes when your boss asks you to stay late on your daughter's
> third birthday, the cake still warm on the kitchen table.
>
> She stopped asking you to come home after that, and you spent the next ten
> years learning how to be invited back.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if needed)
and validates it first: UTF-8 text, 1–280 characters, exactly two lines,
each a single sentence. It then re-reads the file and checks it is
byte-exactly the intended entry.
